Basketball is a team sport played by two teams. The main goal is to score points by shooting the ball through the opponent's basket.

History of Basketball

Basketball was invented by James Naismith in 1891. He created the game as an indoor activity for students during the winter

Important Events

  1. Basketball was invented in 1891.
  2. The sport became popular in the United States
  3. Basketball became an international sport
  4. The NBA was established in 1946

Basic Rules

  • Each team tries to score points.
  • Players must dribble while moving with the ball.
  • Players cannot travel with the ball.
  • Physical fouls are not allowed.
  • The team with the most points wins.

Positions

  • Point Guard
  • Shooting Guard
  • Small Forward
  • Power Forward
  • Center

Why Basketball Is Popular

Basketball is popular because it is exciting, competitive, and can be played by people of different skill levels. It is also widely played in school, communities, and professional leagues.

Conclusion
